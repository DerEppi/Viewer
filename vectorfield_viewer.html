Display — layer toggles

Turn each layer on or off. An active layer gets its own settings section below.

FieldThe velocity field as markers at each grid point.
StreamlinesLines / particles that follow the field.
GridThe bounding box wireframe.
AxesLabelled X/Y/Z axes with ticks.
StarsThe real stars (opens the Stars section).
Density CloudGlowing cloud of the stellar mass (or kinetic energy) density. Strength grows and brightens the points; Intensity is a gain on the alpha, not a 0–100 % opacity — above 1 it mainly lifts the faint outskirts.
ObjectsLiterature objects around Taurus (see Reference Objects at the end).
StyleButton in the header: colour presets for the 3D view, each shown with a preview of its colours. A preset sets the background, the three axis colours (and their labels), the grid box, the label text and the light/dark tint of the interface — nothing else; colormaps, star colours and the 2D plot windows keep their own settings. Screenshots and videos come out as shown.
Custom styleThe last entry of that list is editable: pick it and the list grows a row per colour — Background, X/Y/Z axis, Grid box, Labels — each a swatch that opens the same colour picker used everywhere else in the viewer, applied live. Interface switches its panels between dark and light. It starts as a copy of the preset that was active when you picked it, and is saved with the other settings.
Reference Frame

Which velocity is subtracted from the field before display. All modes start from the same raw measurement.

Gaia (raw)No subtraction — the field as measured, in the Sun's rest frame.
TaurusSubtracts Taurus's bulk motion → internal motions relative to the cloud.
LSRSubtracts the Sun's motion relative to the Local Standard of Rest (Schönrich et al. 2010).
CustomSubtracts a velocity you type in (vx, vy, vz in km/s).

The PMRA/PMDec/RV components (Field → Show as) follow the selected frame too.

Mask

The mask is the region the reconstruction is constrained in — a mass-weighted kernel-density estimate (KDE) of the stars, cut at a threshold. Field markers, streamline seeds and the density cloud are limited to it, hence its own section.

Cut levelThreshold on the KDE density; updates the view live while dragging. The read-out shows the level and the surviving voxel count — double-click it to type an exact level. ⟲ returns to the exported default. Auto colorbar ranges follow the mask; the total-energy read-out deliberately stays on the fixed default mask so it remains comparable.
BandwidthWidth of the KDE kernel, as the factor the sample covariance is scaled by: Exported reuses the exported density verbatim (factor 0.20), Custom puts the factor on a slider and recomputes the density in the browser — smaller resolves single clumps, larger smooths the complex into one blob.
StarsGroups opens the Star groups window; Apply to mask then builds the KDE from the shown groups only (e.g. one SigMA cluster). Fewer than four stars can't define a density — the last one is kept and the status line says so.
Field
Velocity / Curl / Div / StdWhich quantity the markers show: velocity, curl (rotation), divergence (expansion/contraction) or local spread. Streamlines always follow the velocity.
Show asVector = markers pointing along the field; magnitude or a single X/Y/Z (or PMRA/PMDec/RV) component = coloured scalar markers.
Marker shapeButton next to Show as: arrows ↔ cones for vectors, crosses ↔ diamonds for scalars. Each mode remembers its own shape.
SizeSize of the markers.
SubsampleDraw only every Nth marker (1 = all) — less clutter, faster.
Hover / ClickHover shows a marker's value; click opens the full voxel record in the active reference frame.
MaskWhich voxels carry markers is set in the Mask section, not here.
Streamlines
Random seedsStatic lines from random starting points; opacity shows local speed.
AnimatedParticles flowing along the field — faster where the field is stronger.
CountHow many streamlines / particles to show.

Stars
Display modeThe button at the top cycles Dots → UVW vectors (full 3D velocity, needs a measured rv) → PM vectors (tangential velocity from the proper motion alone, rv set to 0 — available for every star).
Marker / Vector sizeIn a vector mode: shape (arrow with shaft ↔ plain cone) and length scale. Arrow length always encodes the speed.
MaskWhich stars are shown: All stars / Has RV / RV used in inference — applies to dots and both vector modes.
Color byProperty the stars are coloured by (age, rv, group, … or Uniform with a colour picker) — the same in all three display modes. The speeds are just two of the fields: Velocity magnitude (UVW) and (PM only). Stars without a value are grey; the ∅ button hides them.
Membership fieldsLuhman / SigMA / Custom group are categorical: every group gets its own colour and a legend (swatch, name, count) replaces the colorbar.
GroupsOpens the shared Star groups window (same one from the Stars section, the star plot window and Mask): which groups are visible. Hiding a group removes its stars from the 3D view, both 2D plots and every count and auto range at once. The tabs at the top pick the grouping, all / none set everything; the button is marked ● while a filter is active. Saved between sessions.
Custom groupsThird tab of that window — groups you draw yourself. + new group switches to selection mode: right-drag a box (left-drag still rotates), click a star to add/remove it, Enter creates, Esc leaves (and only the selection). Started from the star plot window it is drawn in that plot: in a scatter as a box over the points, in a histogram over the bars — a click takes a whole bar, a right-drag sweeps several, and the marked share of every bar is shown in the marker colour. Rename ✎ / delete ✕ in the window. Saved by Gaia source ID.
Overlapping groupsCustom groups may overlap — a new group no longer takes its stars from the older ones. A star stays visible as long as any group it belongs to is shown, and every group's row counts all of its stars. For the colour and the legend one name has to win: that is the newest group the star is in.
Find IDFlies the camera to a star by Gaia source ID (several comma-separated) and marks it with an orange ring. Clicking a star opens its full data card.
Noise estimate ηPer-star rv noise factor of the inference: the fit used σeff = σrv·√η, so η ≈ 1 means "rv error as quoted", η ≫ 1 means the rv was down-weighted (possible outlier/binary). Only for stars whose rv entered the fit; colour in 3D with log₁₀ η (η is heavy-tailed). The Residuals group also has residuals in units of σ and, for rv, of σ·√η.
Colorbars & value ranges

Every colorbar (Field, Streamlines, Cloud, Stars, and the 2D plots) carries the same controls.

Range handlesDrag the two handles to show only objects inside that value range. The hidden/greyed button picks what happens to the rest; ⟲ or a double-click resets.
min / maxClamp the colour scale by hand (blank = auto for that end). ⇄ reverses the scale, Auto returns both ends to the data, ⤓ adopts the current handle window as min/max (it then spreads over the whole colormap and the handles reset).
∅Objects without a value are never touched by the range handles — this button shows them grey or hides them.
StripThe thin band under each colorbar sketches how the values are distributed along the scale.
Data & Field plots (2D)

Two floating windows: ◱ Data plots (Stars section — the real stars) and ◱ Field plots (Field section — the mask voxels, live in the active frame). Both can be open at once and are drawn into PNG/PDF exports as shown.

HistogramDistribution of one field. Left-drag selects an x-range (data and axis are cropped), right-drag pans, wheel zooms. Fit overlays Gauss / Log-norm / Power law / Exp / KDE — formula below the plot, parameters inside it.
Fit rangeButton at the right end of the Fit row: restricts the fit to an x-range while the whole histogram stays drawn (fit a core, judge the tails). Type the bounds or take them from lines / from axis; one bound is enough. The excluded part is dimmed, so a restricted fit is recognizable in exports. Changing the field clears the bounds.
Scatter 2DTwo fields against each other, optionally coloured by a third. Wheel zooms, drag pans (right-drag: x only), double-click resets. Click a star point to pin it; pinned IDs can be copied.
Axis range ⋯The ⋯ box in each axis row (scatter only) opens a min/max editor; an active override shows as mn–mx. Zoom/pan works on top — ⟲ full range / double-click returns to these ends.
⟲ (axis)Left of each axis dropdown: resets manual range, log and flip of that axis (histogram x also the drag-selected range).
⇄ / logPer-axis flip and log scale. Log only engages for strictly positive data; an active log axis is marked (log) in its label.
Color bySame controls as the 3D colorbars: range handles, hidden/greyed, min/max row with ⇄ / ⤓ / Auto, and the ∅ button.
Groups(Star window) Opens the same Star groups window the 3D view uses. Starting + new group from here draws in this plot: a box over the points in the scatter, or bar by bar in the histogram (click = one bar, right-drag = several). Switching between the two mid-drawing keeps what is already marked.
Star positionsGalactic x/y/z [pc] are available as plot axes and colour field (2D only — in 3D they would just paint a gradient).
Power spec.(Field window) Spherically averaged 3D FFT power spectrum, log-log on logarithmic k-shells. |v| = total E(k) = ½ Σ|FFT(vc)|², vx/vy/vz = that component's Ec(k) (they sum to the total), scalars = |FFT(f)|². + spectrum overlays another field as a second curve, ✕ all clears them. (Binning and slope lines follow Hutschenreuter et al. 2026.)
Lines+ line adds a dashed reference line: one value field with an x = / y = toggle and a colour. In the power spectrum it is a slope line instead (value = log-log slope, default Kolmogorov −11/3), drawn through the low-k end. Chips next to the button edit or remove single lines, ✕ all removes all. Each mode keeps its own set; lines follow zoom/flip/log and appear in exports.
Isochrones(Star window) In a colour-magnitude diagram (BP−RP or G−RP vs abs. G mag) an Isochrones row appears: + isochrone overlays a pre-main-sequence isochrone of a chosen age (0.5–15 Myr), colour-coded and labelled in a legend. Curves from BHAC15 (Baraffe et al. 2015, Gaia bands).
StyleButton next to Grid: preset list for the 2D plots (theme, seaborn, matplotlib-light, …). Colormap sits under Color by; grid, bin count and plot colour keep their rows.
Screenshot & video

The ⬇ button opens the screenshot panel. Only the 3D field is captured — never the sidebar or HUD. Colorbars can be attached to the right edge; unchecking everything exports the plain render.

The 360° video can instead fly through your camera bookmarks (Camera path → Bookmark tour): tick at least two bookmarks (visited in list order); the duration slider sets the seconds per leg.

About this Visualisation

This viewer shows a 3D velocity field of the Taurus star-forming region, reconstructed from stellar proper motions and radial velocities using NIFTy (Numerical Information Field Theory). A regular grid is placed around the data, divided into voxels of 3 × 3 × 3 pc each — every voxel carries an inferred 3D velocity vector. A density mask derived from kernel density estimation of the stellar positions marks which voxels actually contain stars; this lets you isolate the physically relevant regions from the surrounding empty grid. The streamlines are purely a visualisation aid — they trace paths through the inferred velocity field and are not physical trajectories.

Beyond viewing the field in 3D, the viewer doubles as an analysis tool for individual aspects of the field and the underlying data: velocities can be shown in different reference frames (raw / Taurus / LSR / custom) or projected back into the observables PMRA/PMDec/RV; besides velocity, derived fields (curl, divergence, uncertainty, density, kinetic energy) can be displayed and the total kinetic energy is integrated live. Every voxel and star is clickable for its full data record, stars are searchable by Gaia ID, and interactive colorbar range filters isolate value ranges directly in 3D. The plot windows (Field plots / Star plots) add histograms with distribution fits, scatter plots and power spectra — with reference lines, log axes and PNG/PDF export. Camera bookmarks, screenshots and video recording round it off.

Reference Objects

Real astronomical objects around Taurus, shown for spatial context. They sit outside the grid (zoom out to see them) and are coarse literature positions — not part of the reconstruction. Sources: star-forming regions from Zhou, Li & Chen (2025); Musca/CrA/Pipe from Zucker et al. (2020, 2021); Per-Tau shell from Bialy et al. (2021); Orion-Eridanus after Joubaud et al. (2020) and Pon et al. (2014, 2016); Local Bubble from Pelgrims et al. (2020); dust map from Edenhofer et al. (2024).

LabelsObject names in 3D. The Label size slider scales them 1×–5× — turn it up to keep names readable when zoomed out.
SunThe Sun, at the origin (0, 0, 0).
TaurusTaurus's catalogue position and velocity as a single point (the grid itself is the full reconstruction).
RegionsNearby star-forming regions (Lupus, Ophiuchus, Chamaeleon, Perseus, Orion, λ Ori) as points, with optional velocity arrows.
+ Musca/CrA/PipeThree more nearby clouds — position only, no velocity data.
Per-Tau ShellA spherical shell (radius ~78 pc) between Perseus and Taurus.
Orion-EridanusA rough stand-in ellipsoid for the superbubble — illustrative shape only, no agreed geometry exists.
Local BubbleThe irregular dust-traced cavity around the Sun. The detail slider picks its resolution.
Dust mapLocal 3D dust density around the Sun as a soft cloud. Opacity/size sliders adjust it.
Save & load settings

Your setup is kept in this browser automatically, per file. To carry it elsewhere — another computer, another run, or simply to keep several views side by side — save it as a small .json file and load it back when needed.

The file holds everything the viewer remembers: visible layers, all slider values, reference frame, colormaps and colorbar ranges, mask level and KDE settings, star colouring and group filters, both plot windows, camera position and camera bookmarks. Loading a file replaces the current setup and reloads the page.

Files can be exchanged between runs: options that do not fit the other data set are skipped, the rest applies.

Reset

Restore every viewer option (visibility toggles, sliders, colormaps, reference frame, colorbar ranges, camera, …) to its default and clear the saved layout for this file.
