3D velocity field of the Taurus molecular cloud complex

Interactive viewer for a velocity field reconstructed from Gaia DR3 astrometry and radial velocities using Bayesian information field theory (NIFTy). Runs entirely in the browser — no installation, nothing is uploaded.

Features

  • Reference frames — heliocentric, relative to the Taurus bulk motion, the LSR, or a custom vector.
  • Colour by anything — velocity components, divergence, vorticity, posterior uncertainty, stellar age, mass, membership group.
  • Adjustable mask — move the density threshold to see where the field is constrained by data and where it extrapolates.
  • Streamlines and 2D plots — animated tracers, histograms, scatter plots, colour–magnitude diagrams, power spectra.
  • Export — screenshots and videos.
Part of a bachelor's thesis on the kinematics of the Taurus star-forming region. Source: github.com/DerEppi/Viewer
