3D velocity field of the Taurus molecular cloud complex

Interactive viewer for a velocity field reconstructed from Gaia DR3 astrometry and radial velocities using Bayesian information field theory (NIFTy). Runs entirely in the browser — no installation, nothing is uploaded.

What you can do in the viewer

  • Rotate and fly through the reconstructed field; show it as vectors, cones or streamlines.
  • Switch the reference frame between heliocentric, the Taurus bulk motion, the LSR or a custom vector.
  • Colour field and stars by any quantity — velocity components, divergence, vorticity, posterior uncertainty, stellar age, mass, extinction, RUWE, membership group.
  • Move the mask threshold to see how far the reconstruction is actually constrained by data.
  • Open 2D plots (histograms, scatter, colour–magnitude diagrams, power spectra) alongside the 3D view.
  • Export screenshots and videos with colorbars composited in.
